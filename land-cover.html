Land Cover Change Detection (2000-2020)

20-year Landsat time series analysis
Google Earth Engine R Random Forest NDVI

Overview

Analyzed 20 years of Landsat 5, 7, and 8 imagery across 10,000 sq km. Applied Random Forest classification in Google Earth Engine. Calculated NDVI time series and identified land cover transitions using post-classification comparison.

Results

Quantified 15% forest loss, 22% urban expansion, and 8% agricultural conversion.
