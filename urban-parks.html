Urban Park Accessibility Analysis

Network analysis evaluating pedestrian access to parks
ArcGIS Pro Network Analyst Python PostGIS

Overview

Network analysis evaluating pedestrian access to parks for 2+ million residents across 150 neighborhoods. Used ArcGIS Network Analyst to generate 10-minute walking service areas. Calculated population served using Census block data. Identified 23 underserved neighborhoods.

Results

Results informed $4.2M in city park investment and were presented to city council.

Park Service Areas

10-minute walking service areas from park entrances across 150 neighborhoods.
